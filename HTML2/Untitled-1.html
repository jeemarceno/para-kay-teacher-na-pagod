If Only You Knew

Sometimes, the feelings we keep inside are the ones that deserve to be heard.

Dear You,

I don't really know where to begin, because sometimes words are not enough to explain what someone makes you feel.

There's something about you that makes ordinary moments feel special. Your smile, your laughter, and even the little things you do stay in my mind longer than you know.

I find myself looking forward to talking to you. Even a simple conversation with you can make an ordinary day feel better.

I like you. Not just because of how beautiful you are, but because I genuinely want to know the person behind that beautiful smile.

I don't want to rush anything. I don't expect you to feel the same way immediately. I just want to be honest about what I feel.

I want to know your dreams, your fears, your favorite things, and even the stories you don't usually tell anyone.

Maybe this is the beginning of something beautiful, or maybe it's simply a feeling I needed the courage to express.

But if life gives me the chance, I'd love to be someone who makes you smile as much as you unknowingly make me smile.

Sincerely,
Someone who genuinely likes you. ♡
